# Store Operations

## About American Backyard
American Backyard is a family-owned business located in Alpharetta, just outside North Point Mall. American Backyard began in 1992 under the name 'Georgia Backyard.' Today, it is one of the largest outdoor furniture stores in the state, boasting nearly 25,000 square feet. We showcase the latest and greatest styles in the outdoor furniture industry and strive to sell the highest-quality products at competitive prices. Our staff is always dedicated to providing thorough, efficient, and satisfying service to all of our customers. We carry good-quality, affordable furniture for any outdoor area, whether that's the pool, patio, deck, porch, or screened-in living space. American Backyard also sells grills, fire pits, and gas logs, including the latest infrared grills and smokers. With over 50 years of patio industry experience, American Backyard will provide you with the shopping experience that only a family-owned expert company can. Serving Georgia's metro Atlanta area: Alpharetta, Roswell, Milton, Cumming, Suwanee, Duluth, Sandy Springs, Brookhaven, Dunwoody, Buckhead, Marietta, Gainesville, Canton, Kennesaw, Woodstock, Smyrna, Jasper, Ellijay, Athens, Greensboro, Mineral Springs, Peachtree City, Jonesboro, Cartersville, and more.

## Payment

## Pickup
Pickups take place at the store, located at 310 North Point Cir, Alpharetta, GA 30022. Pickups can occur anytime during store hours, but customers should call ahead so their order can be prepared. Please let us know at the time of ordering, whether in-store or over the phone, that you intend to pick up your furniture. Pickups typically work best when customers have a pickup truck or other large vehicle to transport their furniture. Customers are welcome to bring trucks or trailers. We kindly ask that customers with trucks, trailers, or larger orders park on the right side of the building, close to the big loading dock door. Customers with smaller orders, such as two chairs or small accessories, can park in front of the store and come inside to receive their furniture. American Backyard is not able to provide moving blankets, straps, equipment, or tools for customers to take home. However, we will gladly help bring your furniture to your vehicle and load it.

## Offered Services
Our staff offers free CAD design services — a computer-rendered layout of your outdoor space with your chosen furniture pieces or sets — and will help you curate the perfect outdoor living space, from picking out furniture to deciding where and how to set it up. We can also help you find the perfect set or piece for your space. Our staff can help with certain repairs on older furniture, whether it was purchased from us or elsewhere. To find out whether a repair is possible, contact or visit the store and show us the piece. We also offer warranty help on older furniture purchased from American Backyard — contact the store to find out whether your piece is eligible for warranty service.

## What We Sell (and What We Don't)
American Backyard specializes in outdoor living: patio furniture (dining sets, deep seating, sectionals, chaise loungers, Adirondacks, love seat swings, rockers, pool chairs, pool chaises), umbrellas and shade, outdoor cushions and pillows, grills, cleaning and maintenance products, ottomans, furniture covers, umbrella covers, grill covers, firepit covers, rugs, fire pits, and gas logs. 

American Backyard does not sell hot tubs, spas, boats, boat care, green egg grills, or lawn care.
