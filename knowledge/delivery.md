# Delivery Information

## Delivery Basics
American Backyard offers delivery, provided and conducted by our own staff at varying prices depending on the location and nature of the delivery. Typically, deliveries involve the American Backyard delivery crew arriving with the customer's product in a truck. The delivery crew will greet the customer, then begin moving or assembling the furniture where it needs to go, per the customer's instructions. The delivery crew can assist the customer in deciding where to place furniture if the customer wants a second opinion. The customer can also ask the delivery team to move other furniture pieces around. Delivery times typically default to a window between 10am and 2pm, but this is subject to change depending on the day and the number, nature, and location of deliveries. The default delivery window can also be changed due to special instruction by the customer (this involves setting a specific time or time interval for delivery). American Backyard's delivery team will call to announce any changes to their estimated time of arrival. Deliveries are offered all over Georgia, and some specialty orders can be made for bordering states such as South Carolina, Tennesee, Alabama, or Florida.

## Scheduling a Delivery
When a customer places their order with American Backyard staff, whether in person, over the phone, or online, the customer can expect a call later to confirm a delivery time. Upon ordering, the customer will share their email, phone number, address, and any specific instructions that may be relevant to the future delivery (specific times to deliver by, days you cannot receive a delivery, a date you would prefer to get furniture by, codes for gates or gated neighborhoods, and more). Once American Backyard has received a customer's furniture — or a couple of days beforehand — a staff member will call the customer to schedule a delivery date and time. On the day of the scheduled delivery, the delivery team will also call half an hour before arriving at the customer's house (if a customer would prefer to be called earlier, they should specify this in the special instructions for the delivery).

## Pricing a Delivery
Pricing varies based on the customer's location. The customer should confirm delivery costs by contacting our staff at the store phone number, 770-754-5750, or the store email address, support@americanbackyard.com. More distant locations or deliveries with unusual circumstances can affect the delivery fee, so the only accurate way to determine pricing for a delivery is to call the store and speak with a staff member.

## Problems with a Delivery
Customers should take the initiative to check all of their furniture for damage and confirm that the product is what they ordered. Customers should also give direction to the delivery team and follow along to ensure no damage occurs to their furniture or home during delivery. If a customer believes there is damage or a problem with their order, they can either tell the delivery driver (who will call the store so the issue can be discussed further) or call the store directly to make the issue known. Our staff will work diligently to resolve whatever problem is found.
